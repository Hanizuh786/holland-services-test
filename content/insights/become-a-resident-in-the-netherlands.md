This page supports visitors who are not simply leaving the UAE but actively planning residence in the Netherlands. The decision may involve family, tax, healthcare, housing, business ownership and timing.

It connects to the Leaving the UAE page but focuses on the destination side: what should be prepared before arrival, which documents may need legalisation and when tax residence or registration questions should be considered.

The page is written for orientation and lead generation, not as a government instruction manual.
